# 🕰️ Trinity Reveal Timeline

> **Complete Arc of Trinity Discovery Across 59 Cases**  
> This document tracks when and how each Trinity member is revealed, and the narrative beats that guide player awareness.

---

## 📊 Overview

The Trinity (الثالوث) consists of three master criminals who engineer crimes as psychological experiments:

1. **The Clockmaker** (صانع الساعات) - Timeline/logistics manipulation
2. **The Alchemist** (الخيميائي) - Forensic evidence tampering
3. **The Whisperer** (المُلقّن) - Psychological manipulation via Dr. Yahya

**Critical Secret:** The Trinity currently has only **2 active members**. The third seat is vacant and being prepared for the player.

## ⚠️ Critical Narrative Guardrails

1. **Never reveal all three members before Case 40**
   - Pace revelations to allow gradual understanding
   - Each reveal should answer one question and raise two more

2. **The vacant seat must remain ambiguous until Case 53**
   - Players should suspect a third member is dead
   - But NOT realize they're being groomed to replace them

3. **Dr. Yahya's betrayal must feel earned, not cheap**
   - Plant at least 10 subtle hints across cases 01-43
   - Hints should be noticeable in hindsight but not obvious initially

4. **Case 58 revelation must recontextualize everything**
   - Players should replay cases and see the "interview" pattern
   - Each case's difficulty and focus should make sense as evaluation criteria

5. **Multiple endings must feel equally valid**
   - No "correct" choice — each ending reflects player's moral philosophy
   - Game should not judge player's decision

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

> "The Trinity should feel like they're **watching the player**, not just the detective.  
> Every UI anomaly, every delayed report, every witness disappearance —  
> these are responses to the **player's actions**, creating a sense that the game itself is alive and reactive."

**The ultimate goal:** Make the player feel that their intelligence is both their greatest weapon and their greatest burden. The smarter they are, the more the Trinity responds, and the heavier the moral weight becomes.
